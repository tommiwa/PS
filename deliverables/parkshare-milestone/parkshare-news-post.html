(DevSecOps) ParkShare Reaches $20,000 in Total Spend

ParkShare connects employees who need workplace parking with available spots provided by BCI and shared by colleagues.

What is ParkShare?

ParkShare is BCI’s workplace parking app. It provides one place for employees to find and book available parking, including spaces provided by BCI and spots shared by colleagues.

Using ParkShare, a colleague can make their spot available for a date when they do not need it. An employee looking for parking can view available options—whether offered by BCI or another employee—and confirm a booking. This makes better use of parking that might otherwise sit empty while giving colleagues who share their spots a way to recover value from them.

What it does

By bringing BCI-provided and colleague-shared spaces into one service, ParkShare helps employees find parking and helps spot owners share their space when it is available. The app supports workplace access without requiring employees to coordinate individual arrangements outside the program.

Having another parking option close to the office can be especially useful for employees arriving early or leaving late.

That practical exchange has now carried ParkShare to an important milestone.

One program, more useful parking: employees can find a space when they need one, while available BCI and colleague-shared spots are put to practical use.

A $20,000 milestone

On August 31, 2026, ParkShare passed $20,000 in cumulative spend. In the five months and 15 days since March 16, 148 unique participants completed 1,427 confirmed and completed bookings through the app.

Measured from March 16 to August 31, 2026—a span of five months and 15 days.

The activity represents $20,012.61 in total spend, including $7,244.61 in peer-to-peer revenue for spot owners. In practical terms, ParkShare has turned parking availability that could otherwise have gone unused into more than $20,000 in completed bookings—making an existing workplace resource more useful for employees.
