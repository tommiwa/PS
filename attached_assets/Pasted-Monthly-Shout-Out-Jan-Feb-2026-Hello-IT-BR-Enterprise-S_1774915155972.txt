Enterprise Legal Tracker

Technology and Legal Operations partnered to build an automation that introduces a new approach to metrics tracking for the Enterprise Legal team. The solution leverages AI (Claude) to interpret and process tasks arriving in Legal inboxes, eliminating the need for manual data entry, saving 5-10 hours of manual effort per month. Legal team members simply forward task assignment emails to a designated inbox. Claude's natural language processing capabilities analyze the email content to extract key task details (Internal Counsel, Work Type, Clients, Summary, deadlines, etc.), populate relevant fields to inform metrics reporting, automatically create properly formatted entries in the Legal Affairs task tracker and assign tasks to the appropriate team member. Please reach out to Thomas Yu if you have any questions.

Smart Shield Implementation

Successfully implemented Kainos Smart Shield in our Workday Sandbox and Preview Workday environments to ensure sensitive data and documents remain protected during Project Keystone testing and configuration activities. Smart Shield is a Workday overlay that automatically masks and protects confidential information (i.e. compensation, performance data, personal employee details) and documents from unauthorized users while proxying as other people/roles. Proxy functionality is essential for testing and configuration, but without Smart Shield, users could inadvertently access data and documents they shouldn't see. This tool ensures everyone can do their jobs effectively while keeping sensitive information and documents secure. Please reach out to Jonny Trirogoff if you have any questions.

S&P/TSX Index Restatements for Dec 30 – Jan 5th

This project was executed for Finance, ISR, Capital Markets and Technology. In early January, the vendor for the TSX Index and constituent data issued a restatement with corrected data, spanning multiple days. Downstream systems in Performance, CM&CI and other groups were affected, as they require accurate data for fund management and reporting. Given the scope of this restatement, it was very time sensitive to reload, master and publish the corrections to enable end of year reporting to proceed. As part of this effort, new functionality was implemented to the EDM index constituent export solution pipeline to improve turnaround for such large-scale restatements in the future. Please reach out to Yan Lyesin if you have any questions.

Primary Exchange Pricing

Updated the daily Bloomberg pricing process to source Equity prices from the primary exchanges. Previously it was using Composite Id pricing, which occasionally resulted in discrepancies during certain market events such as after-hours trading. This change aligns all prices with Finance's pricing directive and ensures consistency with front office pricing expectations when valuing the securities and funds in Simcorp. This improvement reduces manual reconciliation efforts for Finance and significantly reduces price ID overrides for the MIITS team resulting in cleaner, more accurate data that is fit for purpose. Please reach out to Johnson Lam if you have any questions.
